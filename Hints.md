## Dead Drop

<details>
  <summary>Hint</summary>
  There are three prime numbers hidden in the image of a clock. Multiply these.
</details>
<details>
  <summary>Answer</summary>
  The three primes are 23 (shown on the clock), 67, and 37 (the width and height of the image). These multiply together to 57017.
</details>
